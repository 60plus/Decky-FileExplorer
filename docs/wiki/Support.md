# Support FileExplorer

Support is optional. All FileExplorer features stay free.

Open **Decky → FileExplorer → Support** (the red heart), then choose a method.
Each method opens its own QR card so it stays readable on a PC or handheld.
The Back action returns to Support; another Back returns to the plugin menu.

[![Buy me a beer](../../images/support/buy-me-a-beer.svg)](https://buymeacoffee.com/60plus)

**Buy me a beer** uses [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/60plus).
Scan the code with your phone, use **Open page** for Steam's browser, or **Copy link**.

For cryptocurrency, scan the address with a wallet or use **Copy address**:

The cryptocurrency codes contain only the address, with no preset amount.
QR codes are bundled with the plugin; viewing them does not contact a QR service.
The light card keeps them readable regardless of your appearance settings.
